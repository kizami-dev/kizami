# Premium rate for overtime work exceeding 60 hours a month

Where overtime work in one month **exceeds 60 hours**, the premium wage rate for **the portion
exceeding** that figure becomes **50% or more** (proviso to Article 37(1) of the Labor Standards
Act).

## The portion up to 60 hours is unchanged

Only the portion exceeding 60 hours goes to 50%; the portion up to 60 hours stays at the ordinary
premium rate for overtime work (25% or more). It is not a case of "everything becomes 50% once the
month passes 60 hours".

| Category of overtime work | Premium rate |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 60 hours | 25% or more |
| Portion exceeding 60 hours | 50% or more |

## The grace period for small and medium-sized enterprises has ended

This 50% rule applied to large companies from April 1, 2010, but small and medium-sized enterprises
had a grace measure. Since **April 1, 2023** it applies to small and medium-sized enterprises as
well, so the same rule now applies regardless of company size.
