# The obligation to take five days per year

A person granted 10 or more days of annual paid leave in a year has to take **5 days within one
year of the grant date**. This is both a right of the worker and an obligation imposed on the
company.

## Watch how it is counted

| Unit of leave | Does it count toward the 5 days? |
| --- | --- |
| Full day | Counts as 1.0 day |
| Half day (morning or afternoon) | **Counts as 0.5 days** |
| Hourly units | **Does not count** |

Annual paid leave taken in hourly units is not accepted as leave taken for the purpose of meeting
this obligation of five days. Even if 40 hours are taken in hourly units, the obligation remains
fulfilled to the extent of 0 days. To meet the obligation, the leave has to be taken as a full day
or a half day.

## The deadline

The deadline is "one year after the grant date". Grant dates differ from person to person (where
they are based on the date of hire), so deadlines differ as well. You can check your own deadline
and remaining days on the annual paid leave screen in KIZAMI.

Notifications are sent as the deadline approaches (90 days before and 30 days before).
