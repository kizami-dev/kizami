# Post-closing amendment

When a correction request or a leave request for an already closed month is approved, **just that one change**
can be applied and the affected user's totals recalculated without unlocking the closing for the whole month.
The month stays closed.

## The difference from the original figures remains

Applying a post-closing amendment puts the month into the "amended after closing" state. Both the original
figures as of the closing and the current figures after the amendment are retained, and the monthly screen
keeps showing the **difference from the original figures**. Which categories changed and by how much can be
traced afterwards.

## How this differs from unlocking a closing

| Operation | Scope of the effect | State of the month |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unlocking a closing | Makes the whole month freely editable | Returns to unconfirmed |
| Post-closing amendment | Applies only the one approved change | Stays confirmed (the difference is recorded) |

Because it avoids reopening the month, a post-closing amendment suits changes with a small scope. Where the
whole month needs to be reviewed, unlock the closing instead.
