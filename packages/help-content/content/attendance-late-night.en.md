# The premium for late-night work (22:00 to 5:00 the next morning)

Hours worked between **10:00 p.m. and 5:00 a.m. the next morning** are treated as late-night work and are
subject to premium wages of **25% or more** on top of ordinary wages (Article 37(4) of the Labor Standards Act).

## Overlapping premiums are added together

Late-night work can occur at the same time as overtime work or work on holidays. In that case the premium
rates are **added together**.

| Combination | Approximate premium rate |
| --- | --- |
| Late-night work only | 25% or more |
| Overtime work + late-night work | 50% or more (25% + 25%) |
| Work on a statutory holiday + late-night work | 60% or more (35% + 25%) |

## What KIZAMI covers

KIZAMI goes as far as classifying and totaling the hours that fall in the late-night band (22:00 to 5:00 the
next morning) from the punches. Calculating and paying the actual premium wages is done on the payroll side,
based on those totals.
