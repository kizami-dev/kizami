# 特例措置対象事業場 (workplace covered by the special measure — 44-hour week)

Statutory working hours are 40 hours per week in principle, but a workplace that meets **both** of
the following conditions has **44 hours**.

1. Its industry is one of **commerce, the motion picture and theater business, the health and hygiene business, or the amusement and entertainment business**
2. It regularly employs **fewer than 10 workers**

This was established when the 40-hour week was fully implemented in 1997, and it is **a system that
is still alive today**, not a transitional measure from the past.

## Effect on aggregation

Statutory working hours per week are used to calculate the
[total working hours limit under the flexible working hours system](./attendance-flex-frame), so
whether or not a workplace qualifies changes the monthly total hours limit.

| Days in the month | 40-hour week | 44-hour week |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 30 days | 171 hours 25 minutes | 188 hours 34 minutes |
| 31 days | 177 hours 8 minutes | 194 hours 51 minutes |

For a 30-day month the difference is roughly 17 hours. **If a workplace qualifies but the setting
is not applied, work that is not actually overtime is recorded as overtime.**

## Setting it and reviewing it

You switch this in "Settings → Tenant profile". The determination is made per workplace, so **a
workplace stops qualifying as soon as it has 10 or more employees**. Review the setting whenever
the headcount changes (KIZAMI does not determine this automatically from the headcount).
