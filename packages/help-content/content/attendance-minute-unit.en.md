# Rounding of working hours (units of one minute)

As a rule, working hours are calculated from the daily record in **units of one minute**. Treatments that
round the hours down to less than what was actually worked and thereby reduce wages, such as "discard
anything under 15 minutes" or "discard anything under 30 minutes", may be held to violate Article 24 of the
Labor Standards Act (full payment of wages) or Article 37 (payment of premium wages).

## The only exception: rounding the monthly total

The daily working hours themselves cannot be rounded, but where a fraction of less than one hour arises in the
**monthly total of overtime work, work on holidays and late-night work**, and only there, it is permitted to
round down fractions of less than 30 minutes and round up fractions of 30 minutes or more
(昭63.3.14基発150号, Notification Kihatsu No. 150 of March 14, 1988).

| Subject | Rounding |
| --- | --- |
| Daily working hours | Cannot be rounded down (calculated in units of one minute) |
| Monthly total of overtime work and the like | Fractions under 30 minutes may be rounded down and 30 minutes or more rounded up |

It is often assumed that "daily punches may be rounded", but what is permitted applies only to the monthly total.
