# Template for internal terms of use on time punches

A template for internal terms of use is available from the "Settings > Personal information"
screen, covering the duty to punch accurately, the ban on punching on someone else's behalf, the
procedure for a correction request when a punch has been missed, and the handling of fraudulent
punches.

Use the template to suit how your own company operates, whether by making it part of the rules of
employment as it stands or by publicizing it to employees as a separate document. If you have set a
link to your rules of employment, a pointer to them is appended automatically at the end of the
template.
