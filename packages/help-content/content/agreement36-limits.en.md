# Upper limits under the Article 36 agreement

To have workers perform overtime work or work on holidays, a 36協定 (Article 36 agreement, a labor-management agreement) must be concluded and filed in advance.
The hours by which working time may be extended are subject to upper limits backed by penalties (Article 36(4) to (6) of the Labor Standards Act).

## General rule (limit on extended working hours)

| Period | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Month | 45 hours |
| Year | 360 hours |

## Special clause (where temporary special circumstances exist)

Where it is necessary to have workers work beyond the general limits, hours may be extended up to the limits
below, on condition that **an Article 36 agreement with a special clause has been concluded and filed in
advance**. Without a special clause, this extension is not permitted.

| Period | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Overtime work per year | 720 hours or less |
| A single month (including work on holidays) | Less than 100 hours |
| Multi-month average (average over 2 to 6 months, including work on holidays) | 80 hours or less |
| Number of times a month may exceed 45 hours | Up to 6 times per year |

A special clause is meant to be used only in months where there are "temporary special circumstances";
it is not intended to permit working up to the maximum limits on an ongoing basis.
