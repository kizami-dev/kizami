# Unlocking a closing

Unlocking a monthly closing makes the finalized attendance records for that month editable again.

- Requires the "Unlock closing" permission
- If the month has already been exported for payroll, make sure the export destination stays consistent
- Who unlocked it and when is kept in the closing history and visible in the audit log
