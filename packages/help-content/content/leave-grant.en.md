# Statutory grant days and expiry of annual paid leave

A worker who has been **continuously employed for six months** from the day of hiring and has
**reported for work on at least 80%** of all working days during that period is granted annual paid
leave (Article 39(1) of the Labor Standards Act). After that, the number of days increases with
years of service (paragraph (2) of the same Article).

## Days granted by years of service (full-time)

| Years of service | Days granted |
| --- | --- |
| 6 months | 10 days |
| 1 year 6 months | 11 days |
| 2 years 6 months | 12 days |
| 3 years 6 months | 14 days |
| 4 years 6 months | 16 days |
| 5 years 6 months | 18 days |
| 6 years 6 months and beyond | 20 days |

In every band, the condition is that the worker reported for work on at least 80% of all working
days in the period concerned.

## When the number of prescribed working days per week is small (proportional grant)

Workers whose prescribed working hours are under 30 per week AND whose prescribed working days are
4 or fewer per week (or, where the period is other than a week, 216 or fewer prescribed working days
per year) are covered by a separate table of **proportional grant** days rather than the table above
(Article 39(3) of the Labor Standards Act, Article 24-3 of its Enforcement Regulations).

| Days per week | 6 months | 1 yr 6 mo | 2 yr 6 mo | 3 yr 6 mo | 4 yr 6 mo | 5 yr 6 mo | 6 yr 6 mo and beyond |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4 days | 7 | 8 | 9 | 10 | 12 | 13 | 15 |
| 3 days | 5 | 6 | 6 | 8 | 9 | 10 | 11 |
| 2 days | 3 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 6 | 7 |
| 1 day | 1 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 3 | 3 | 3 |

KIZAMI computes the days from this table according to each member's "annual leave grant class".
The class is **not** derived automatically from weekly hours or days: an administrator sets it based
on the rules of employment and the employment contract (member management screen). Members with no
class set are treated as standard (5 or more days per week).

Note that even a proportional grant is **subject to the 5-day mandatory taking obligation once a
single grant reaches 10 days or more** (for example, 3 years 6 months in the 4-days-a-week class).

## Expiry is two years

Annual paid leave that has been granted expires by prescription **two years after the grant date**
(Article 115 of the Labor Standards Act). Only leave within those two years carries over from the
previous year to the current year as unused leave.
