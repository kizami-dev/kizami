# Automatic switchover for statutory amendments

KIZAMI manages statutory rules such as the Labor Standards Act as "versions with an enforcement
date". If you register a future amendment in advance, before it takes effect, the rules switch over
to the new ones automatically the moment the enforcement date arrives, and the amendment is shown
in advance on the tenant profile screen as an upcoming change.

## Past periods keep the rules of their time

An amendment applies only to periods on or after its enforcement date. Not only for months that
have already been closed, but also for past months that have not been closed, the statutory rules
used for aggregation are **the ones that were in force during that period**. Past periods are never
recalculated retroactively under the latest rules.

This prevents past aggregation results from changing every time the law is amended (and diverging
from figures that have already been closed).
