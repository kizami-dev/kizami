# Template for the privacy notice to employees

Time punch records, IP addresses, user agents, and GPS coordinates (where enabled) are personal
information of employees. The obligation to announce to employees the purpose of acquiring this
information and how long it is retained (Articles 17, 18, and 21 of the Act on the Protection of
Personal Information) rests with **the company that deploys the product**, not with the KIZAMI
project.

From the "Settings > Personal information" screen, KIZAMI automatically generates a privacy notice
template that reflects the current tenant settings (whether GPS is enabled or disabled, and the
retention period). If GPS is disabled, the items relating to location information are not shown.

## How to use it

1. Review the text generated on the "Settings > Personal information" screen
2. Revise it to match your own circumstances (such as the contact point for requests for disclosure or correction)
3. Publicize it to employees (by posting it, publishing it on the intranet, attaching it to employment contracts, or another method of your choosing)

The generated text is only a template and is not legal advice. If you have any doubts about its
content, check with an expert such as a certified social insurance and labor consultant or a lawyer.
