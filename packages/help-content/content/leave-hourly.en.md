# Annual paid leave in hourly units

Annual paid leave is in principle taken in units of one day, but **only where a labor-management
agreement has been concluded** may it be taken in hourly units.

## The cap is the equivalent of five days per year

Leave may be taken in hourly units **only up to the equivalent of five days per year**. This is the
statutory ceiling: a labor-management agreement may set a lower figure, but it cannot exceed this.

The number of hours making up "five days" is calculated by **rounding the prescribed working hours
of one day up to a whole hour**.

| Prescribed working hours | Per day | Five days per year |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 8 hours | 8 hours | 40 hours |
| 7 hours 30 minutes | **8 hours** (rounded up) | 40 hours |
| 7 hours | 7 hours | 35 hours |

Annual paid leave carried over from the previous year and taken in hourly units is also counted
**within the current year's allowance of five days**. Carried-over leave does not get a separate
allowance.

## It does not count toward the obligation to take five days

Leave taken in hourly units is not accepted as leave taken for the purpose of meeting the
[obligation to take five days per year](./leave-mandatory-five-days). To meet that obligation, the
leave has to be taken as a full day or a half day.
