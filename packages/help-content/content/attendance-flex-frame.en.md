# The total hours limit under a flexible working hours system

Under a flexible working hours system, working hours are judged not day by day but on the **total for the
settlement period (one month with this setting)**. The upper limit of hours to be worked in that period is
called the total working hours limit (総枠).

```
Total hours limit = statutory weekly working hours × calendar days in that month ÷ 7
```

Statutory weekly working hours are 40 hours as a rule. By way of exception they are 44 hours for workplaces
in commerce, the film and theater industry, health and hygiene services, or the hospitality and entertainment
industry that regularly employ fewer than 10 workers
([特例措置対象事業場](./tenant-special-provision) — a workplace covered by the special measure).

| Days in the month | At 40 hours per week | At 44 hours per week |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 30 days | 171 hours 25 minutes | 188 hours 34 minutes |
| 31 days | 177 hours 8 minutes | 194 hours 51 minutes |

Hours actually worked in excess of the total hours limit are **overtime work**, and hours below it are a
**shortfall**. They are shown as the "Flex balance" on the KIZAMI monthly screen.

## How days of paid leave are treated

A day on which annual paid leave is taken counts toward the actual hours as if it had been worked (the
prescribed working hours for a full day, half of that for a half day, and the corresponding hours for hourly
units). Taking paid leave never increases the shortfall.
